The knowledge base feature lets you write an answer once and share it with every customer using the portal. If you have faced this problem where customers would keep on asking and raising tickets regarding the same issues this is exactly where a knowledge base would come in handy. Instead of answering the same question multiple times, simply create an article in the knowledge base and publish it to your audience.  

Each answer is an article, saved as an **HD Article** record in a category (**HD Article Category**) which would help users to navigate through your articles. Customers read articles in the knoweldge base where they are prompted to type their issue and if an solution exists? the problem is solved there itself. The portal also suggests articles when customers search or raise a ticket, so fewer customers need to raise one.

## Write Your First Article

1. Click **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Create**, then **Article**.
3. Enter a title and the content. Use headings, because the portal lists them under **On this page**.
4. Next to the author name, select a category.
5. Click **Create**. The article is a draft, so only agents can see it.
6. Click **Publish** at the top right.
7. In **Publish article as**, select **Customers** and click **Publish**. Logged-in customers can now read the article.

## See It in the Customer Portal live

Click your brand name at the top left, then click on the **Customer portal** option. Click **Latest** on the home page: your article is at the top of the list. Type a word from the title in the search bar to find it.
