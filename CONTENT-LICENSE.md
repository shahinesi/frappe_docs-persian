# License for documentation content

The current [Frappe License and Trademark policy](https://docs.frappe.io/legal/others/license-and-trademark) states that Frappe documentation is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA), but does not specify a version. The translated Markdown in `content/fa/` and source Markdown snapshots retained under `sources/` follow those applicable CC BY-SA terms. Each translated page identifies its canonical source and states that it is an unofficial translation. The manifest records per-page source attribution and license status.

Do not infer a CC BY-SA version that the source policy does not name. Logos, product icons, photographs, illustrations, videos, and other branded or visual assets are not included unless their separate license is verified. A note on a translated page identifies media omitted for this reason.

The pre-existing `frappe_docs/` tree and the repository's `license.txt` are preserved as historical upstream content and software licensing. They do not replace or broaden the license applicable to live documentation or its translations.
